Your own record, kept before it softens.

Private to you, and end-to-end encrypted: your dreams are locked in your browser with a passphrase only you hold, so we store nothing but ciphertext and cannot read them ourselves. Never sold, never used in research unless you opt in. Sealed versions stay exactly as they came.

Capture, before it softensseal the raw version first

The first words matter most. A seal fixes them to a moment in time and cannot be moved. It proves what you wrote and when, not that the words match the dream: nothing can prove that. Capturing early is how you keep that gap small. You can add a clearer version later; this raw one stays locked. Want to turn a dream into a testable prediction instead? Seal it with a condition →
