Beaded is the one I would combine with Orbit. It gives the struck-seal, minted-coin feeling you were reaching for with Milled, but the rounded beads sit comfortably in the soft, dreamlike world of the site instead of fighting it, and they stay legible shrunk to a favicon. Points is the sparser, more instrument-like alternative. Say the word and I will make it the mark everywhere.
