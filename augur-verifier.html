What this page actually does
  • Rebuilds the payload. It assembles the dream text, the declared claim, the nonce, and the sealed time into one canonical form, exactly as the seal did.
  • Recomputes SHA-256. It hashes that payload in your browser and compares it to the fingerprint in the bundle.
  • Checks the anchor. If the bundle carries an RFC-3161 timestamp token, it parses that token here in your browser and confirms an independent time-stamping authority certified this exact fingerprint, and reads the time it did so. For full assurance, the same token verifies with openssl ts -verify.
  • Reports plainly. Match means the words are original and time-fixed. Mismatch means something changed. No middle ground, no trust required.
