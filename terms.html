02 · What Oneiratory isan instrument, not an oracle

An instrument, not an oracle

Oneiratory lets you record a dream and seal it, which fixes its exact wording to a point in time and proves the text has not changed since. That is the entire technical claim. A seal proves anteriority, that words existed before an event, and nothing more.

Oneiratory takes no position on whether dreams can foresee the future. Outcomes shown in the Registry reflect user submissions and community judgement, not scientific proof of precognition. Nothing on this site is a prediction, a diagnosis, or advice. If you are struggling, please reach out to a qualified professional or a local support service.

03 · Your content, privacy & datawhat happens to what you write

What happens to what you write, and the data we hold

  • The Vault, and your sealed dreams, are private. Both the Vault and the Seal flow are end-to-end encrypted in your browser with a key derived from your passphrase, so the server stores only ciphertext and cannot read a private entry or a sealed dream until you choose to reveal it. Forget the passphrase and that content is unrecoverable, by design, not even by us.
  • The Registry and Commons are public and anonymous by default. Treat anything you post there as visible to everyone. Do not include names, addresses, or details you would not share openly.
  • You own your dreams. By posting to a public space you grant Oneiratory a non-exclusive licence to store, display, and distribute that content within the Service, and for the research uses described in section 05. You keep ownership; this licence exists only so the Service can function.
The personal information we hold

To sign you in we store your email address and a session token. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. To keep the boards usable we apply rate limiting, and we deliberately do not store raw IP addresses. Sealed dreams and Vault entries are held only as ciphertext, as above. Your email address is shared with our email provider for the sole purpose of sending your sign-in link (see section 09).

Your privacy rights

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. You may ask us to access or correct the personal information we hold about you, or to close your account and delete its data, by writing to contact@oneiratory.com. Some material that has already been published in an anonymised, public, or third-party context may not be able to be recalled (see sections 05 and 06). If you have a privacy concern we cannot resolve, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

05 · Researchscholarly use

Research & scholarly use

A registry of timestamped dreams is genuinely useful to the study of dreaming, memory, and prediction. To make that possible:

By submitting a dream to the public Registry or the Commons, you grant permission for that content, in anonymised form, to be used for academic and scientific research, and to be quoted or cited in scholarly journals, books, and conference publications.

This permission covers only anonymised, publicly submitted material. Your private Vault entries are never included in research without your explicit, separate opt-in.

Wherever practical, researchers are given aggregated data or short excerpts rather than whole entries, and identifying details are stripped before any dataset leaves Oneiratory. You may request removal of your public submissions at any time via contact@oneiratory.com. Please note that material already published in a third-party scholarly work cannot be retracted by us after the fact.
