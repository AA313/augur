How AUGUR works, what happens to what you write, and the permissions that let this become useful to research. Written to be read, not endured.

In short
  • AUGUR proves when a dream was written, not that dreams predict anything.
  • Your Vault is private. The Registry and Commons are public and anonymous.

Reaching a human

02 · What AUGUR isan instrument, not an oracle

An instrument, not an oracle

AUGUR lets you record a dream and seal it, which fixes its exact wording to a point in time and proves the text has not changed since. That is the entire technical claim. A seal proves anteriority, that words existed before an event, and nothing more.

AUGUR takes no position on whether dreams can foresee the future. Outcomes shown in the Registry reflect user submissions and community judgement, not scientific proof of precognition. Nothing on this site should be treated as a prediction, a diagnosis, or advice.

03 · Your content & privacywhat happens to what you write

What happens to what you write

  • The Vault is private. In the full service, entries are encrypted so that only you can read them. In this prototype, Vault entries live only in your browser's memory and are never stored or transmitted.
  • The Registry and Commons are public and anonymous by default. Treat anything you post there as visible to everyone. Do not include names, addresses, or details you would not share openly.
  • You own your dreams. By posting to a public space you grant AUGUR a non-exclusive licence to store, display, and distribute that content within the Service, and for the research uses described in section 05.
04 · Conductanonymous, not lawless

The Commons is anonymous, not lawless

Post in good faith. Do not submit content that is illegal, that harasses or targets others, that incites hatred or violence, or that exposes another person's private information. Impersonation and spam are not welcome. We may remove posts or threads, and restrict access, to keep the Commons usable and safe.

05 · Researchscholarly use

Research & scholarly use

A registry of timestamped dreams is genuinely useful to the study of dreaming, memory, and prediction. To make that possible:

By submitting a dream to the public Registry or the Commons, you grant permission for that content, in anonymised form, to be used for academic and scientific research, and to be quoted or cited in scholarly journals, books, and conference publications.

This permission covers only anonymised, publicly submitted material. Your private Vault entries are never included in research without your explicit, separate opt-in.

Wherever practical, researchers are given aggregated data or short excerpts rather than whole entries, and identifying details are stripped before any dataset leaves AUGUR. You may request removal of your public submissions at any time via contact@augur.app. Please note that material already published in a third-party scholarly work cannot be retracted by us after the fact.
