Why Oneiratory existsa note from the founder

I did not build Oneiratory for the money.

I started it to bring together people whose dreams sometimes arrive early, or quietly come true, so that no one has to feel strange, alone, or judged for what they have seen. For a long time I carried these experiences without knowing who to tell. Oneiratory is the place I wish I had found: somewhere honest, where you can record a dream, share it if you want to, and see plainly that you are not the only one.

The other reason was research. If enough of us keep an honest, timestamped record, it could grow into something larger than any one person: real, dated, verifiable data that helps us understand dreams, and whether they can genuinely reach into what is coming, in a way scattered stories never could. That possibility mattered to me as much as the community did.

What keeps it runningno ads, no data sold

Oneiratory carries no advertising, and it will never sell or leak a dream. That independence is the whole point of the project, and it does carry a real running cost: hosting, a domain, and the time it takes to moderate the Commons and keep everyone safe.

A donation is simply the alternative to advertising and data-selling, never an addition to them. If Oneiratory is worth something to you, and you would like to help keep it online, free, and independent, a contribution of any size is genuinely appreciated.

Give via stripe PayPal Ko-fi
A one-off gift, or whatever you feel is right. Whichever you choose, it is secure and goes straight to keeping Oneiratory online.

There is no obligation to give. Nothing here is ever locked behind a payment. Every part of Oneiratory, reading, sealing, posting, and verifying, works exactly the same whether you donate or not. If you cannot give, or would simply rather not, that is completely fine. You are just as welcome here.

Other ways to help
  • Share it. If you know someone who has had a dream come true and felt they could not tell anyone, point them here.
  • Post honestly. Record the misses beside the hits. An honest record is the one thing this project cannot buy, and the one thing that makes it worth anything.
  • Add to the record. Every sealed dream, resolved openly, makes the community warmer and the research stronger for everyone who comes after.
